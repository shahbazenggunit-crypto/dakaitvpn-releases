DakaitVPN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

DakaitVPN gives you a private, encrypted connection to the internet. This page explains what we keep and what we do not.

What we do not collect

We do not log the websites you visit, the apps you use, your DNS requests, or the content of your traffic. We do not keep your real IP address in our logs. We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

What we keep, and why

  • A random device ID created by the app, so one Premium plan works on one phone.
  • Your Premium key or Google Play purchase token and its end date, to know whether Premium is active.
  • A VPN public key and a private address inside the VPN (for example 10.8.0.5), needed for the connection to work.
  • The time of the last VPN connection, kept only in memory on the server, to show how many people are online.
  • A push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), so we can send service messages such as maintenance notices or plan expiry reminders.

Ads

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings under Google, Ads. Premium has no ads.

Payments

Purchases in the Play Store version are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Google tells us only whether a purchase is active and when it ends.

How long we keep it

Free session records are removed shortly after the session ends. Premium records are kept while the plan is active and afterwards for our sales records. Email us and we will delete your records.

VPN permission

Android asks you to allow a VPN connection. The app uses this only to send your traffic through the encrypted tunnel to the server you choose.

Children

DakaitVPN is not meant for children under 13.
